# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: Oct 01, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Leftware** ("we",
"us", or "our") handles information in the **Flutter Ads Demo** Android app
(the "App"). The App demonstrates banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads
provided by Google Mobile Ads.

## Information handled by the App

The App does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information.
It has no first-party analytics, advertising profile, or server account. The
reward state shown after a rewarded ad is used within the current app session.

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When ads are requested or shown,
Google and its advertising partners may automatically receive or collect
information such as your IP address, device and app information, advertising
identifier or other device identifiers, ad interactions, and ad diagnostics.
Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and ad configuration, this
information may be used to deliver, personalize, measure, and protect ads. The
exact information and purposes are determined by Google's services and the
settings used for the App; we do not control Google's independent processing.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## How information is used and shared

We do not sell personal information or maintain a separate database of App
users. Information collected through the advertising SDK is handled by Google
and its partners to provide and operate advertising, measure ad performance,
prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations. Google's handling
of that information is subject to Google's terms and policies.

## Your choices

You can manage advertising ID and ad-personalization settings in your Android
device settings. Google also provides controls through [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
The controls available depend on your device and location. The current App
does not include an in-app consent management flow. Before serving ads in
regions where consent is required, the publisher must implement and maintain
an appropriate consent flow; publishing this policy does not replace that
requirement.

## Data retention and security

We do not retain information in an App-operated account or server. Google and
its partners determine how long information collected through their services
is retained, as described in their policies. We take reasonable care in
maintaining the App, but no method of transmission or storage is completely
secure.

## Children

The App is not designed to collect personal information directly from
children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to
us, contact us using the details below. The publisher must configure Google
Play and advertising settings appropriately for the App's actual audience.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will
post the updated policy at this URL and revise the effective date above.
